## The narrative (use in this order)

1. **Loss, not gain.** AI answers "best X near me" hundreds of millions of times a day and the reader never sees your site. You are not losing rankings, you are losing the lead.
2. **Show, do not tell.** "What do the top AI sites say about your business? Find out now." The audit is the hook, because it produces a result in two minutes.
3. **Control.** You cannot edit what ChatGPT says. You can make your site readable and accurate so it stops guessing.
4. **Price anchor.** Listed in front of AI users from $39 a month. Cheap against one lost booking.
