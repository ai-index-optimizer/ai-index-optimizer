## Why one link per channel
Each channel gets its own utm_source so page_view events can be grouped by source. Without it every visitor looks identical and we cannot tell which post produced a signup.

## Measurement gap being closed
As of 18 Nov 2026 the public pages fired no page-view event, so anonymous traffic was invisible in app analytics. The builder has been asked to add page_view plus conversion events and UTM persistence.
